WHY THIS IS MORE ACCURATE

A face embedding, not an image category

Each detected, aligned face becomes a 128-number identity signature. Several varied signatures per person are compared with a strict distance threshold, ambiguity check, and multi-frame confirmation, so an uncertain visitor is labelled Unknown instead of being forced into a family category.
